FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve Central Florida, including Pinellas, Hillsborough, Polk, Orange, Pasco, Manatee, Sarasota and Hernando counties.

What window and door brands do you carry?

We install PGT WinGuard 5500 impact windows, PGT EnergyVue 5400 non-impact windows, Simonton Stormbreaker Plus impact windows, Simonton Collection Series, PGT and Simonton patio doors, French and entry doors, Plastpro doors, and Schlage locksets.

Do you offer free in-home quotes?

Yes. One of our product specialists will gladly visit your home for a free consultation and quote. Call 888-230-0243 to schedule.

Are impact windows worth it in Florida?

Impact windows provide storm protection, may reduce homeowners insurance costs, improve energy efficiency, and add long-term value — making them a strong investment for Florida homes.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. Products are backed by manufacturer warranties (PGT, Simonton, Plastpro), and we include our 3-Year Anniversary Inspection Program for added peace of mind.
