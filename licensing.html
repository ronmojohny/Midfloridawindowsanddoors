Insurance Coverage

An uninsured installer is a risk that lands on your homeowners policy. Here is what we carry and why each part matters to you.

General Liability

Covers property damage and bodily injury arising from our work at your home — a dropped window unit, a damaged exterior wall, a broken fixture. If something is damaged during installation, it is handled through our insurance rather than your claim history.

Workers' Compensation

Covers our crews if a worker is injured on your property. Without it, an injured worker's medical costs can become a claim against you as the homeowner. Florida law requires this coverage at the contractor level, and we maintain it.

Ask any contractor for a certificate of insurance before work begins, then call the phone number printed on that certificate to confirm it is still in force. It takes two minutes and it is the single best protection you have.

Why Licensing & Insurance Matter to You

  • Permits and inspections go through. A licensed contractor can pull permits and pass inspection. Unlicensed work can be red-tagged, forcing removal of the installation at your expense.
  • Your warranty stays intact. Manufacturers and insurers may refuse to honor coverage on work performed or installed by an unlicensed party.
  • Protection if something goes wrong. Contractors without coverage leave the loss with you. Insurance shifts that risk back where it belongs.
  • Recourse through the state. Florida's construction recovery fund and DBPR complaint process are available to you only when the contractor is properly licensed.
  • Wind mitigation credit eligibility. Your carrier will want documentation that openings meet the Florida Building Code. Licensed, permitted installation produces that paper trail.
  • Resale value. Unpermitted window and door work surfaces during a home inspection and often becomes a price concession at closing.

How to Verify Us Yourself

You should never take a contractor's word for it. Both of these checks are free, public, and take a few minutes.

Step 1 — Confirm the license is active

Go to the state's public license search at MyFloridaLicense.com and search license number CGC1533928. Confirm three things: the status reads Current, Active, the business name matches Mid-Florida Windows and Doors, LLC, and there are no disciplinary actions listed.

Step 2 — Confirm the insurance is in force

Ask us for a certificate of insurance. Then call the carrier using the phone number printed on the certificate — not a number we give you — and confirm the policy is active with no lapse. Ask them to mail or email you a copy directly if you want it in writing.

Step 3 — Confirm the track record

Review our profile and customer feedback on the Better Business Bureau. You can also ask us for local references from completed jobs in your county.

Licensing & Insurance FAQ

Are you licensed in Florida?

Yes. Mid-Florida Windows and Doors, LLC holds Florida Certified General Contractor license #CGC1533928, issued by the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation (DBPR). You can look it up yourself at any time on the state's free license search at MyFloridaLicense.com.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry general liability insurance to protect your property and workers' compensation coverage. Certificates of insurance are available on request, and we are happy to have your insurer or agent named as a certificate holder before work begins.

Do I need a permit to replace windows or doors in Florida?

In most cases, yes. Replacement window and door installations typically require a permit, and in Florida's wind-borne debris regions the openings must meet the Florida Building Code. We handle the permit application, the required product approval documentation, and the inspection process on your behalf.

How do I verify your license and insurance before hiring you?

Three easy steps. Search license CGC1533928 on MyFloridaLicense.com to confirm it is active and in good standing. Ask us for a certificate of insurance and call the carrier's number printed on it to confirm coverage is in force. Then check our BBB profile for accreditation status and customer reviews.
